Type a Customer Name and pick from the suggestions (sourced from orders.json) — Material Name then only suggests materials that customer has actually ordered, and Finish / Surface only suggests finishes seen for that customer + material. If a customer has more than one material, pick the first, type a comma, and it'll suggest the remaining ones for that customer — keep adding commas for as many as you need (same trick works for multiple finishes). Delivery Address, Qty, Expected Delivery Time, and Remarks are always left blank for you to fill in. Use the 🎨 swatch on a row to highlight it any color (✕ clears it) — the color prints too. Long text wraps instead of getting cut off when you print. Click 🖨 Print when ready — it's formatted for A4 landscape.
